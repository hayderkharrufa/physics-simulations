# Preset drops

The drop selector offers eight fixed drops, so a whole class can work through the same numbers. The values below are nominal: each measurement varies by about 1%, because the drop is jittered the way a real one is.

| Drop | n | a (µm) | v_t (µm/s) | balance V (V) | v_E at 400 V (µm/s) | v_E at 500 V (µm/s) | v_E at 600 V (µm/s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 0.51 | 27.4 | 150 | 45.6 | 63.9 | 82.1 |
| 2 | 2 | 0.76 | 59.5 | 240 | 39.6 | 64.4 | 89.2 |
| 3 | 3 | 0.96 | 94.4 | 320 | 23.6 | 53.1 | 82.6 |
| 4 | 4 | 0.88 | 80.8 | 190 | 89.3 | 131.8 | 174.3 |
| 5 | 5 | 1.20 | 148.8 | 380 | 7.8 | 47.0 | 86.1 |
| 6 | 6 | 1.14 | 133.8 | 270 | 64.4 | 114.0 | 163.5 |
| 7 | 7 | 1.31 | 176.3 | 350 | 25.2 | 75.6 | 125.9 |
| 8 | 8 | 1.30 | 173.9 | 300 | 57.9 | 115.9 | 173.9 |

Plate separation d = 5.00 mm, so E = V / d: 400 V gives 8.0 × 10⁴ V/m, 600 V gives 1.2 × 10⁵ V/m.

Substituting into q = K (v_t + v_E) / E returns n × 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C for every drop.
